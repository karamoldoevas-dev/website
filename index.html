KYRGYSTAN

The early Kyrgyz people originally migrated from the Upper Yenisei River region in Siberia. By the 9th century, they established the powerful Kyrgyz Khaganate, which dominated vast areas of Central Asia. Over time, successive waves of Mongol and various Turkic empires influenced the region, resulting in a rich blending of cultures.

KYRGYSTAN IS AWESOME
Learn more about Kyrgyzstan

The Kyrgyz people have preserved their nomadic roots for centuries. Central to this lifestyle are horses, which are considered the "wings of man," and the yurt—a portable, felt-covered tent decorated with intricate traditional patterns. Visitors can still experience authentic nomadic hospitality by staying in yurt camps, drinking kymyz (fermented mare's milk), and witnessing traditional horse games like Kok-Boru.
